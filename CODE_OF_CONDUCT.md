# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Give and take criticism about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks or sexualised language or imagery.
- Don't publish anyone's private information, such as their address or email, without their permission.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@ian-white83](https://github.com/ian-white83), through a GitHub direct message or by asking in an issue for a private way to get in touch. Don't put the details in a public issue. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who don't follow this code, in line with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
